The cooperative

A collective project,
grounded in its land.

The Cannabis Abnae Chaaouan cooperative is based in Douar Tisskit, Morocco. It showcases its members’ work and their connection to the Rif region.

Led by its president, Mohamed Chaaouan, it provides a meeting point for professionals, partners and anyone wishing to learn about its work.
